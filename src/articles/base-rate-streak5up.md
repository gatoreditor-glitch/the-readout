Five straight up days feels euphoric -- every morning green, every dip bought, the market seemingly unable to fall. Contrarian instinct screams "overbought." Since 2010 there have been 37 such streaks. Over the next 21 trading days (about a month), the market was higher 84% of the time. But the median gain was just +2.0%.

## The numbers

- **Setup:** the S&P 500 rises five consecutive trading days.
- **What followed:** a median gain of **+2.0%** over the next 21 trading days.
- **Hit rate:** positive **84%** of the time -- 31 out of 37.
- **Sample:** 37 episodes, 2010 through 2026. A large sample.

## What it means

Strength begets strength -- briefly. A five-day winning streak means buyers are in firm control: every seller has been absorbed five days running. That kind of demand doesn't usually vanish overnight, so the market tends to drift higher over the following month. With 37 episodes, this is one of the most reliable patterns in the database.

## The fine print

Read the two numbers together: 84% hit rate, +2.0% median gain. That's high consistency with low magnitude -- roughly a normal month's return, achieved with unusual reliability. This is not a setup that makes you rich; it's a setup that tells you not to fight the tape. Shorting into a five-day streak because it "feels overbought" has been a losing trade 84% of the time. Also note the horizon: the edge is measured over one month. Beyond that, the streak's information decays -- a month later, you're just back to the market's normal drift.

## How to use it

Use this base rate as a discipline device, not a profit engine. When the market rips five days straight and you're tempted to fade it, the data says stand down -- the overbought trade loses far more often than it wins. If you're underinvested, a streak is permission to put money to work rather than waiting for a pullback that usually doesn't come soon. But don't chase aggressively for big gains: the +2.0% median says the reward for piling in late is modest. Let the streak confirm your longs; don't let it seduce you into new ones at any price.
